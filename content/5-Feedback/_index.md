Having gone through the meaningful **First Cloud AI Journey** internship, I would like to share my personal opinions and feedback below to help improve the program for future cohorts.

### General Assessment of the Program

**1. Learning and Working Environment**
The program was organized very professionally and methodically. The learning documentation system is clear, and the detailed roadmap helps interns easily track their own progress week by week. The combination of self-study and workshops maximizes flexibility.

**2. Support from Mentors & the Admin Team**
The Mentor team is highly experienced and enthusiastic. Whenever I ran into difficulties, the mentors always guided me toward the mindset of checking system logs rather than simply handing over ready-made answers, which helped improve my problem-solving skills. The Admin team also provided technical support and issued AWS Free Tier practice accounts very quickly.

**3. Relevance to My Field of Study**
As a **Computer Science** student at Hanoi University of Civil Engineering (HUCE), I found the hands-on content very close to real industry practice. Personally configuring VPC isolation, Public/Private Subnets, setting up Security Groups, Network ACLs, and applying the principle of least privilege with IAM Policies helped me deeply understand how to secure cloud infrastructure.

**4. Learning Opportunities & Skill Development**
The program was a great opportunity for me to get hands-on with modern cloud technologies such as Serverless (Lambda, API Gateway), Containers (Docker, ECS), and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, CloudFormation). Beyond technical skills, I also improved my technical report writing and time management skills.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit**
The knowledge-sharing culture within the AWS Study Group was very active. Everyone was open-minded, unafraid to ask questions, and actively helped one another get through difficult labs.

---

### Survey Responses

- **What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**
  Being given a real AWS account for hands-on practice with actual cloud services, which turned abstract theoretical knowledge into working, real-world products.
- **What do you think the program should improve for future cohorts?**
  Adding a few small weekly study group check-in sessions so interns can interact directly and share common configuration mistakes they run into.
- **Would you recommend this program to your friends?**
  Absolutely. This is the most well-structured, hands-on cloud program that helps students close the gap between academia and industry.

---

### Suggestions & Aspirations

- **Suggestion**: The organizing team could design additional Deep Dive sessions on real-world cloud security incidents, so that Cybersecurity students can gain more practical perspective.
- **Aspiration**: I hope to continue accompanying and participating in the AWS Study Group's community activities in the future in order to keep up with new technologies.
